**Q1.** What problem does this solve?

### Goals and non-goals

**Q2.** What's in scope?

* `tasks.py export --csv` prints every task as CSV to stdout, with a header row `number,title,done` (recommended)

> Answer: agree. Import is a non-goal.

* None

> Answer: agree

### Architecture
